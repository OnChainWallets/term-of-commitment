# COOKIE POLICY

**Last updated:** June 22, 2026

---

This Cookie Policy explains how Horus Technologies LLC, doing business as Horus Edge ("Company," "we," "us," and "our"), uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website at [https://horusedge.com](https://horusedge.com) ("Website") and use our SaaS platform. It explains what these technologies are and why we use them, as well as your rights to control our use of them.

## 1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by website owners in order to make their websites work, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

In addition to standard cookies, our platform heavily utilizes **HTML5 Local Storage and Session Storage**. Because our platform interacts with Web3 components and on-chain data architecture, we use these local storage technologies to securely maintain your authentication tokens, interface preferences, and Web3 wallet connection states (e.g., Coinbase Wallet SDK or similar connectors) across page reloads without sending this data back and forth to our servers.

## 2. Why do we use these technologies?

We use first-party and third-party cookies (and local storage) for several reasons. Some are required for technical reasons in order for our Website to operate securely, and we refer to these as "essential" or "strictly necessary" technologies. Others enable us to track and target the interests of our users to enhance the experience on our platform, or for marketing and analytics.

## 3. Types of technologies we use

- **Essential / Strictly Necessary:** These are vital for the platform to function. They include secure session tokens (so you don't have to log in every time you click a new dashboard), CSRF protection tokens, and local storage keys that remember if your Web3 wallet is currently connected to the interface. _(These cannot be disabled if you wish to use the platform)._
- **Analytics and Performance:** These technologies collect information that is used in aggregate form to help us understand how our Website is being used. For example, we use Google Analytics and Adobe Analytics to see which predictive market dashboards are most popular, helping us improve our infrastructure.
- **Advertising and Marketing:** These are third-party cookies (such as Meta Pixel or Google Ads tags) used to make advertising messages more relevant to you and to track the efficiency of our user acquisition campaigns. They prevent the same ad from continuously reappearing and help us measure ROI on marketing.

## 4. How can you control cookies and local storage?

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our Website, but your access to some functionality and secure areas of our SaaS platform will be severely restricted or broken.

Because our platform relies on Local Storage for Web3 wallet states and secure sessions, you can clear these manually at any time by clearing your browser's cache and site data. Please note that doing so will instantly log you out and disconnect any active wallet sessions on Horus Edge.

For more information on how to manage tracking technologies, visit your browser's settings or visit [www.allaboutcookies.org](https://www.allaboutcookies.org).
