## 1. Nature of Transaction (No Crypto Trading)
* **1.1. Software License Only:** By subscribing to OnChain Wallets, you are purchasing a non-exclusive license to use our data visualization software.
* **1.2. Fiat Currency Priority:** Unless otherwise explicitly stated (see Section 7), all payments are processed in FIAT currency (e.g., USD, EUR, BRL) via credit/debit cards. OnChain Wallets is **NOT** a cryptocurrency exchange, wallet provider, or money transmitter. No cryptocurrency is bought, sold, or transferred during the payment process for our standard credit card subscriptions.

## 2. Subscription Plans and Billing
* **2.1. Recurring Billing:** Your subscription allows access to premium features. By subscribing, you authorize us (via third party payment processors) to charge the applicable fees on a recurring basis (monthly or annually) until you opt out.
* **2.2. Automatic Renewal:** Your subscription will automatically renew at the end of each billing cycle unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

## 3. Upgrades and Downgrades (Proration)
* **3.1. Upgrading Your Plan:** If you upgrade your subscription level (e.g., from Tier 1 to Tier 3) during a billing cycle, the upgrade will take effect immediately. We will charge you the difference between the new plan price and the remaining value of your current plan (pro-rated amount).
* **3.2. Downgrading:** If you downgrade your plan, the change will generally take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

## 4. Modifications to Supported Networks & Services
 * **4.1. Critical Third-Party Reliance:** OnChain Wallets relies on external infrastructure, APIs, and node providers to retrieve blockchain data. You acknowledge and agree that **we may face temporary instabilities, data delays, or the permanent termination of services** for some or all of our services due to changes, failures, or decisions made by these third-party providers or the underlying blockchain protocols.
* **4.2. Protocol Forks & Network Changes:** Blockchain networks are decentralized and subject to "forks," technical migrations, or sudden obsolescence. OnChain Wallets does not control these events. We reserve the right to suspend or permanently discontinue support for any network that becomes unstable, incompatible with our providers, or technically unfeasible to maintain, without prior notice.
* **4.3 Network-Specific Plans (Remedy & Goodwill):** If you have purchased a subscription limited to specific blockchain networks and one or many of those networks become permanently unavailable on our Platform:
    * **User Action:** You must contact us via `support@onchainwallets.io`.
    * **Resolution:** We may, as a gesture of goodwill, offer to migrate your account to another active network of equivalent value or negotiate a pro-rated refund for the remaining unused days of your billing cycle. This pro-rated refund or migration is your **sole and exclusive remedy**.

* **4.4. Full Access Plans:** If your plan grants access to "All Networks" or the complete OnChain Wallets suite, the removal or instability of a specific network does **not** entitle you to a refund or credit. These plans are sold as access to our general platform and its current set of available features; the service is considered fulfilled as long as the Platform remains operational for the majority of its supported chains.

## 5. Cancellation and Refunds
* **5.1. Cancellation:** You may cancel your subscription at any time via your account settings. You will retain access to paid features until the end of your current paid period.
* **5.2. Refund Policy:**
    * **General:** Except as provided in Section 4.3 or required by law, all subscription fees are non-refundable.
    * **EU/UK/Brazil Residents:** Users in these jurisdictions may have specific withdrawal rights (e.g., 7 to 14 days) mandated by local law, provided they have not fully utilized the digital content.

## 6. Failed Payments
6.1. If our payment processors cannot charge your payment method (e.g., expired card, insufficient funds), we may suspend your access to the Pro features immediately until payment is successfully processed.

## 7. Cryptocurrency Payments (Future Provisions)
If and when the Platform supports direct cryptocurrency payments (e.g., USDC, Bitcoin, Ethereum), the following terms apply:

* **7.1. Volatility Risk:** You acknowledge that the value of cryptocurrencies is highly volatile. The amount required for a subscription is fixed in FIAT currency (USD/BRL). We are not responsible for fluctuations in exchange rates between the moment you initiate a transaction and the moment it is confirmed on the blockchain.
* **7.2. Network Accuracy:** You are solely responsible for ensuring you send the correct asset to the correct network address (e.g., sending USDC via ERC-20 vs. SPL). Funds sent to the wrong address or network are permanently lost and will not be refunded or credited.
* **7.3. Refunds in Crypto:** If a refund is legally required for a payment made in cryptocurrency, we reserve the right to refund the original Fiat value of the transaction (according to our base prices in US Dollars), regardless of the current price of the token.
* **7.4. Tax Compliance:** You are responsible for any tax implications of spending crypto in your jurisdiction (e.g., capital gains events upon disposal of assets).
