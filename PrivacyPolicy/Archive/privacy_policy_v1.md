# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last updated:** June 22, 2026

---

This Privacy Notice for Horus Technologies LLC (doing business as Horus Edge) ("we," "us," or "our"), describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services").

Horus Edge is a SaaS data intelligence platform providing analytics for predictive markets utilizing public blockchain data. We provide customizable search tools and on-chain analytics.

**Questions or concerns?** Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [horus@horusedge.com](mailto:horus@horusedge.com).

## 1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

### Personal information you disclose to us

**In Short:** We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services, express an interest in obtaining information about us, or otherwise contact us.

The personal information that we collect depends on the context of your interactions with us. The personal information we collect may include names, email addresses, passwords, and billing addresses.

**Payment Data.** We may collect data necessary to process your payment if you choose to make purchases. All payment data is handled and stored securely by our payment processor, Stripe. You may find their privacy notice link here: [https://stripe.com/privacy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

**No Cryptocurrency Custody.** We process traditional fiat payments for our SaaS subscriptions. We do not host digital wallets, process cryptocurrency transactions, or custody digital assets of any kind for our users.

### Information automatically collected

**In Short:** Some information — such as your Internet Protocol (IP) address and/or browser and device characteristics — is collected automatically when you visit our Services. This information does not reveal your specific identity (like your name) but may include device and usage information needed to maintain the security and operation of our Services.

## 2. PUBLIC BLOCKCHAIN DATA AND WALLET TRACKING

**In Short:** We aggregate, analyze, and display publicly available blockchain data related to predictive markets.

By the very nature of decentralized networks, blockchain data is public, transparent, and immutable. Our platform collects and indexes data from these public ledgers, including but not limited to public wallet addresses (e.g., alphanumeric strings starting with "0x"), transaction hashes, smart contract interactions, trade volumes, and predictive market outcomes.

**No Expectation of Privacy on Public Ledgers:** Wallet addresses and on-chain transaction histories are generally not considered Personally Identifiable Information (PII) under standard privacy frameworks when not linked by us to a specific real-world identity. We display this public data to provide market intelligence (e.g., tracking "Smart Money" or Order Flow Imbalance). You acknowledge that public blockchain data cannot be deleted, modified, or hidden by Horus Edge.

## 3. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

**In Short:** We process your information to provide, improve, and administer our Services, communicate with you, for security and fraud prevention, and to comply with law.

We process your personal information to facilitate account creation and authentication, to deliver and facilitate delivery of services to the user, to respond to user inquiries, and to manage user orders and subscriptions.

## 4. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

**In Short:** We keep your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Notice unless otherwise required by law.

When we have no ongoing legitimate business need to process your personal account information (e.g., your email and billing data), we will either delete or anonymize such information.

**Exception for On-Chain Data:** Because blockchain ledgers are inherently immutable, any analytics, wallet performance tracking, or market breakdowns generated from public on-chain data will remain indefinitely in our database and dashboards, regardless of any user account deletion requests.

## 5. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?

**Account Information:** If you would at any time like to review or change the information in your account or terminate your account, you can log in to your account settings and update your user account. Upon your request to terminate your account, we will deactivate or delete your account and personal information from our active databases.

**Limitation on Right to Erasure:** Please note that the "Right to be Forgotten" or "Right to Erasure" applicable in certain jurisdictions (such as under GDPR or CCPA) applies exclusively to the personal account data (email, name) you provided to us. It **does not apply** to the public blockchain data (wallet addresses, transactions, ROI tracking) that we aggregate from decentralized networks, as we are simply indexing public domain information.
